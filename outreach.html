Outreach & Community Engagement

I am passionate about science communication and community engagement. Below are highlights from my outreach, mentoring, and STEM education activities.

  • Girls in STEM Workshop

    University of Arizona, Tucson | 2025

    Leading a hands-on crater formation activity for middle school students.

    I helped facilitate a workshop introducing young girls to astronomy, planetary geology, and scientific problem solving. Activities included:

    • Impact crater formation experiments
    • Demonstrations of remote sensing tools used in planetary research
    • Mentoring discussions on pursuing careers in STEM

  • Public Lecture: Exploring Water on Mars

    Flandrau Science Center & Planetarium | 2024

    Delivering a community lecture about past and present water on Mars.

    I gave a public talk highlighting the role of geomorphology and radar science in understanding Martian hydrology. Topics included:

    • Mars’ glacial history
    • Midlatitude ice sheets
    • Why studying water on Mars matters for astrobiology

  • Mentoring Undergraduate Researchers

    Lunar & Planetary Laboratory | 2024–Present

    I mentor undergraduate students working on planetary surface processes and geologic mapping. Responsibilities include:

    • Training students to use GIS tools and photogrammetry workflows
    • Co-authoring abstracts for conferences
    • Supporting students’ applications to REU programs and fellowships
